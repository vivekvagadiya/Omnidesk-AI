# ABC Technologies General Workplace & Benefits Policy (2026)

## 1. Flexible Working Hours & Core Collaboration
ABC Technologies supports a hybrid and flexible work culture:
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which team members are expected to be reachable via Slack and available for synchronous meetings.
- Hybrid employees are encouraged to spend at least 2 days per week in their local ABC corporate office.

## 2. Wellness and Learning Allowances
- **Wellness Allowance**: Every full-time employee receives $1,200 annually ($100 per month) for gym memberships, fitness trackers, sports equipment, or mental health counseling.
- **Learning & Professional Development**: An annual stipend of $1,500 is provided for conferences, technical books, certifications (AWS, GCP, Kubernetes), and online coursework.
- Expenses should be submitted via the internal reimbursement portal with itemized receipts.

## 3. Support Tickets & Issue Resolution SLAs
Support requests submitted to IT or HR adhere to the following Service Level Agreements (SLAs):
- **CRITICAL**: Initial response within 1 hour; resolution target within 4 hours.
- **HIGH**: Initial response within 4 hours; resolution target within 1 business day.
- **MEDIUM**: Initial response within 8 business hours; resolution target within 2 business days.
- **LOW**: Initial response within 24 business hours; resolution target within 4 business days.
