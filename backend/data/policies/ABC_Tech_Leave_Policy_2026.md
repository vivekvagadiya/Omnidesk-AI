# ABC Technologies Leave & Attendance Policy (2026)

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlements
All full-time employees at ABC Technologies Inc. receive the following paid time off entitlements per calendar year, credited on January 1st:
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 12 days per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 7 days per calendar year.
- **Maternity Leave**: 90 consecutive calendar days.
- **Paternity Leave**: 15 days paid leave within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
- **Bereavement Leave**: 5 days of compassionate leave for immediate family members.

## 2. Casual Leave Guidelines
Casual leave is intended for personal matters, emergency family events, or short planned vacations.
- Casual leaves cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive working days without prior managerial approval.
- Applications for planned casual leave should be submitted at least 48 hours in advance via the OmniDesk AI copilot or HRMS.
- Unused casual leave balances lapse automatically on December 31st each year; they cannot be encashed or carried forward into the next year.

## 3. Sick Leave Guidelines
Sick leave is provided for medical illness, recovery, or healthcare emergencies.
- Employees should notify their reporting manager as early as possible on the day of illness.
- A recognized medical practitioner's certificate is strictly mandatory for any sick leave extending beyond 2 consecutive business days.
- Sick leave cannot be combined with casual leave unless authorized by HR.

## 4. Maternity and Parental Leave
ABC Technologies provides 90 days of fully paid maternity leave to eligible female employees for up to two children.
- Expectant mothers should inform HR and their manager at least 4 weeks prior to the intended leave start date.
- Male employees are entitled to 15 days of paid paternity leave to support their partner and newborn child.

## 5. Approval Workflow & Human-in-the-Loop Governance
All leave requests initiated through the OmniDesk AI copilot are staged with a 15-minute token TTL.
- The employee must confirm and approve the staged leave card.
- Deductions are committed to the corporate database only upon human confirmation.
- Once committed, an automated notification is dispatched to the employee's direct reporting manager.
